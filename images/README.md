## Tips
- Use warm, earthy tones in your photography — cream, terracotta, amber, wood tones
- Shoot on natural or warm artificial light
- Background options: linen, raw wood, concrete, sand, terracotta tile
- Shoot from multiple angles for each product (the gallery shows up to 3 images per product)
