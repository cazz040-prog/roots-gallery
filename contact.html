Common Questions

Are your dolls authentic Namji pieces?

Our pieces are handcrafted by artisans in Cameroon in the Namji doll tradition. We present them honestly: they are contemporary works made in a living craft tradition, not historical artefacts. Each comes with a provenance card describing its materials and origin.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. All orders are carefully packed and insured during transit. Shipping times range from 5–14 business days depending on your location and chosen shipping method.

Can I order a specific size or colour combination?

We can sometimes accommodate special requests for specific palette preferences or sizing. Please contact us before placing an order to discuss what is possible. Note that exact replication of a bead pattern is not possible — each piece is unique.

Do you offer wholesale or interior design pricing?

Yes. We work with interior designers, hotels, retailers, and galleries. Please use the contact form or email us directly with details of your interest and we will respond with a wholesale or trade pricing structure.
